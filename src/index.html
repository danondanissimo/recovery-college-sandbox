How to enrol:

  1. Click on the Workshops button on the front page.

  2. Click on your county for in-person workshops or click on the Online button for online workshops.

  3. Choose the workshop from a calendar.

  4. Fill in and submit the form.

OR

  • Call us at 086 1746330.

How to cancel:

  • Edit your submitted form and choose "No" for the "Are you still planning to attend?" question (accessible via the link sent to your email).

  • OR

  • Call us at 086 1746330.
